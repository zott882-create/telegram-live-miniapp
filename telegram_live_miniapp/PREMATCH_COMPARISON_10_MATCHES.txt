PREMATCH COMPARISON — LAST 10 MATCHES

Prematch match cards now have four tabs:
1. Сравнение
2. Матчи
3. Таблица
4. Тренды

The comparison is calculated from each team's last 10 completed API-Football fixtures.
It includes W-D-L, goals for/against, averages, goal difference, O1.5/O2.5/O3.5,
BTTS, clean sheets, failed to score, first-half results, form, and a separate
home-host / away-guest split within those ten matches.

The first successful result is saved in PostgreSQL service state for six hours.
Opening or switching the tab reads cached data; pressing refresh can force a new fetch.
The frozen notification_bot directory was not modified.
