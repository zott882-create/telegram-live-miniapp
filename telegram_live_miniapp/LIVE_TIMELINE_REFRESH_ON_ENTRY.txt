LIVE TIMELINE REFRESH POLICY
============================

- The API-Football event timeline is refreshed on every new opening of a LIVE match card.
- Opening/switching UI tabs inside the same card continues to use the card's in-memory tab cache.
- When a fixture is final, the backend performs one final events request.
- That final response is saved in PostgreSQL forever, including an empty event list.
- Every later opening of the completed match reads PostgreSQL/Redis and makes no API-Football events request.
- Prediction cards still read their saved IGScore snapshot and do not create an on-click external request.
- The notification_bot subtree is unchanged.
